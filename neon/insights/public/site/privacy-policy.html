Privacy Policy.

How DashPatrol collects, uses and protects your information. Last updated November 3, 2026.

ON THIS PAGE 1. What we collect 2. How we use it 3. Google data 4. Sharing 5. Security & retention 6. Your rights 7. Contact

1. What we collect

Account details you give us (name, email, password), the website you connect, and the settings you choose. When you connect Google Search Console or Analytics, we receive performance data for your site. We also collect basic usage data such as pages viewed in the app and device type.

2. How we use it

We use your information only to run DashPatrol for you: researching searches, improving your pages, drafting articles, measuring results and sending your reports. We may email you about your account and product updates. You can opt out of nonessential emails at any time.

3. Google data

Data from Google APIs is used only to provide and improve features for your own site. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train models for anyone else. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect Google access at any time from your account or your Google settings.

4. Sharing

We never sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us run DashPatrol (such as hosting, email and AI processing), under contracts that require them to protect it, or when required by law.

5. Security & retention

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is limited to people who need it. We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your data within 30 days, except where we must keep it by law.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your information at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email info@dashpatrol.io or visit our contact page.
