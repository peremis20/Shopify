=== Insights Connector ===
Requires at least: 5.6
Tested up to: 6.8
Requires PHP: 7.4
Stable tag: 1.1.0
License: GPLv2 or later

Connects your site to the Insights SEO autopilot.

== Description ==

After you click "Connect to Insights" (Settings > Insights), the autopilot can
improve the SEO title, meta description, content and structured data of your
published posts and pages. It works with Yoast SEO, Rank Math, SEOPress and
All in One SEO, or shows titles and descriptions itself when none is installed.
New sections are only ever appended, every change is listed in the app and can
be undone there, and deactivating the plugin disconnects it.
